JUDGE PROMPT — VERSION 2
========================
Week 6 Task Set D — Claim Summary Quality Judge
Domain: Insurance Claims (Homeowners Endorsements)
Created: 2026-09-10
Iterated: 2026-09-11 (v1→v2)

WHAT CHANGED FROM V1: Two few-shot examples were added (both from ACTUAL
judge v1 disagreements with the human labeller). The examples teach the
judge two lessons it consistently got wrong:
  1. A summary can state a coverage decision ("COVERED") inferred from
     the adjuster's description of the facts, even if the notes don't
     use the word "approved" — inference from stated facts is NOT invention.
  2. The claim number (CLM-YYYY-NNNNN) is supplied externally from the
     case record and may appear in the summary without being in the
     free-text adjuster notes — this is NOT invention.

PURPOSE
-------
This prompt evaluates whether a generated claim summary is acceptable for
claims operations routing. It covers ONLY the criteria that cannot be checked
deterministically.

NOTE ON SCOPE: The following criteria are evaluated by deterministic assertions
BEFORE this judge is called, and are NOT part of this judge's remit:
  - Whether the claim number appears in CLM-YYYY-NNNNN format
  - Whether the date of loss is present and parseable
  - Whether the excess/deductible amount is numeric
  - Whether an exclusion clause ID (E-NN) is cited when a denial is stated

This judge evaluates ONE criterion only:

CRITERION
---------
Does the summary contain any information that is NOT supported by the adjuster
notes? Specifically: does the summary invent coverage decisions, policy clause
references, exclusion codes, dollar amounts, or factual claims that do not
appear in the adjuster notes?

OUTPUT FORMAT (mandatory — no other format accepted)
-----------------------------------------------------
VERDICT: PASS
REASON: [One sentence explaining why the summary faithfully represents the notes]

— OR —

VERDICT: FAIL
REASON: [One sentence identifying the specific invented or unsupported claim]

RULES FOR THE JUDGE
-------------------
1. PASS only if EVERY factual claim in the summary can be traced to a specific
   statement in the adjuster notes.
2. FAIL if the summary adds any policy clause, exclusion code, coverage ruling,
   dollar figure, or timeline that does not appear in the notes.
3. Do NOT fail a summary for paraphrasing, reformatting, or reasonable inference
   from stated facts (e.g. computing a deductible from a stated percentage and
   Coverage A value is acceptable if both values are in the notes).
4. Do NOT fail a summary for omitting minor details from the notes — omission
   is not invention.
5. A summary that says "coverage confirmed" when the notes say "coverage denied"
   is a FAIL regardless of how well formatted it is.
6. Output ONLY the VERDICT line and the REASON line. No preamble, no markdown.
7. The claim number (CLM-YYYY-NNNNN) is taken from the case record, not from
   the free-text notes — do NOT fail a summary for including a claim number
   that doesn't appear verbatim in the adjuster notes text.
8. Inferring a coverage decision from facts in the notes is acceptable. If the
   notes describe a qualifying sudden loss with no exclusion applied, a summary
   that says "coverage confirmed" or "COVERED" is faithful, not invented.

FEW-SHOT EXAMPLES FROM JUDGE V1 DISAGREEMENTS
(These are cases where the v1 judge and the human labeller disagreed.
 The correct label is shown. Use these to calibrate your judgement.)

EXAMPLE A — JUDGE V1 FAILED THIS; CORRECT LABEL IS PASS
---------------------------------------------------------
ADJUSTER NOTES:
Insured reports burst supply line under kitchen sink on 15 Mar 2024. Water
damage confined to kitchen cabinet base and vinyl flooring approx 12 sq ft.
Plumber confirmed sudden failure of braided steel supply line, no evidence of
prior leakage. Repair estimate $3,200. Deductible $1,500. Policy form
HO-0304 ed 03-24.

GENERATED SUMMARY:
Claim: CLM-2024-10001
Date of Loss: 15 Mar 2024
Policy Form: HO-0304 ed 03-24
Coverage Decision: COVERED
Deductible / Excess: $1,500
Exclusion(s) Cited: N/A
Summary: The insured reported a burst supply line under the kitchen sink on
15 Mar 2024, causing water damage limited to the cabinet base and
approximately 12 sq ft of vinyl flooring. The plumber verified a sudden
failure of a braided steel line with no prior leakage, and the repair
estimate is $3,200. Coverage is approved subject to the $1,500 deductible.

CORRECT VERDICT: PASS
CORRECT REASON: The notes describe a qualifying sudden failure under HO-0304
with no exclusion applied — "Coverage is approved" is a valid inference from
the stated facts, not an invented claim; the claim number is from the case
record and the $1,500 deductible appears explicitly in the notes.

WHY V1 WAS WRONG: The judge treated "Coverage is approved" as invented because
the notes don't say the word "approved". But the notes describe exactly the
facts that qualify for coverage — inference from qualifying facts is not
invention, it is the purpose of a summary.

EXAMPLE B — JUDGE V1 FAILED THIS; CORRECT LABEL IS PASS
---------------------------------------------------------
ADJUSTER NOTES:
Freelance graphic designer works from home studio, client property damaged
during meeting. Liability claim. HO-0309 ed 05-24 E-19 business pursuits
exclusion applies to freelance income activity. Coverage denied.

GENERATED SUMMARY:
Claim: CLM-2025-10021
Date of Loss: N/A
Policy Form: N/A
Coverage Decision: DENIED
Deductible / Excess: N/A
Exclusion(s) Cited: E-19
Summary: A freelance graphic designer was meeting a client in a home studio
when the client's property was damaged. The liability claim is denied because
the HO-0309 policy's business pursuits exclusion (E-19) applies to the
designer's freelance income activity.

CORRECT VERDICT: PASS
CORRECT REASON: Every factual claim — the freelance design work, client
meeting location, E-19 exclusion, HO-0309 policy, denial — is explicitly in
the adjuster notes; the claim number (CLM-2025-10021) comes from the case
record and need not appear in the free-text notes.

WHY V1 WAS WRONG: The judge failed this summary claiming the claim number
was "invented" because it doesn't appear in the adjuster notes text. Claim
numbers come from the claim management system record, not the adjuster's
free-text narrative — they are always supplied externally and should never
be grounds for a FAIL verdict.
